# Changelog

## November 8, 2026

### New

- The HUD now defaults to the touch screen, on still leaves taken from the main menu, with the columns evenly spaced and the Left/Right touch buttons in from the edge. If you've already played, your settings keep the HUD where it was: switch it on the Game page.
- Infinite items on the Mods page: ammo, homing ammo, orange grenades, crystal coconuts, film and instrument energy stay full once you have them.
- Digital controls in the arcade games: in Donkey Kong and Jetpac the D-pad works as the joystick. The Circle Pad still works.
- Screenshots are numbered and never overwrite each other, and the "saved" message clears itself after 10 seconds.
- The HOME prompt now quits with X instead of A, so a stray A press can't close the game.

### Fixed

- Bonus barrel games in 3D: Kremling Kosh, Teetering Turtle Trouble, Krazy Kong Klamour and Peril Path Panic no longer pop too far out of the screen.
- Gyro aiming: looking up and down now turns the aim by the angle you tilt the console, in first person and the cannon, and the gyro adds to the stick instead of taking over when the stick is centered.
- King Kut Out's voice being cut off during his fight. (Should help with audio cutoff in other areas also)
- The white tops on Gloomy Galleon's gold stacks.
- The main menu's leaves stopping short of the left edge of the screen.
- The touch-screen HUD now hides during fades and zipper transitions and comes back once they've opened.
- DK's head covering the colored banana on the touch-screen HUD.

## November 3, 2026

### New

- Sound works out of the box, now with the game's reverb: no more dumping your DSP firmware.
- Four translations on the Mods page's new Language setting: French, German and Spanish (by Ballaam) and Brazilian Portuguese (by brigandier).
- HUD on the bottom screen if you want it, so the top screen is all game: every counter of the level stays up there with the current kong's icons, plus the health melons. It starts on the top screen; switch it on the Game page.
- Bonus barrel crosshairs in 3D: in Kremling Kosh, Searchlight Seek and the other shooting games the crosshair now sits at the depth of whatever it's over, like the first-person one. The new Crosshair setting allows you to put it at the HUD's depth instead.
- Remap any button, add up to two buttons on the touch screen, and set each screen's brightness. Tag Anywhere's "Mod buttons" setting is gone: to switch kongs with the D-pad, map D-pad left and right to D-Left and D-Right on the Controls page.
- Quitting from the HOME button keeps your save.
- The Luma3DS Rosalina menu now works while playing.
- A Performance monitor toggle on the Game page (frame rate and CPU load on the touch screen).

### Fixed

- The HUD sitting far behind the screen with the 3D on, in levels with a gradient sky (Fungi Forest, Gloomy Galleon, Creepy Castle, DK Isles and the main menu).
- The first-person crosshair jumping far away when aimed at the sky: it now keeps its last depth.
- Holes in models in one eye with the 3D on.
- Fairy camera photos at 60 fps on a New 3DS.

### Old 3DS / 2DS

- Faster: 15-25 fps, up from 15-20.
- Music keeps its tempo: no more slowing down with notes coming late.
- Faster loading with the CIA, which now holds the whole ROM in memory (extended memory mode - the console restarts when you quit).
- Smoother play: wireless and background services pause while the game runs; slight CPU allocation increase.
- A new Models setting draws the kongs with their lighter multiplayer models, on Low by default on an Old 3DS.
